Experience

Tagly: Co-Founder & Sole Engineer

Jan 2025 – Sep 2026

Tagly was a two-sided marketplace where local businesses (cafes, restaurants, gyms) paid their customers for posting about them on Instagram. A customer walked in, ordered a coffee, took a picture and posted it to their Instagram story. Then they opened Tagly and linked the story to that business. After 24 hours, they earned a reward. $100,000 raised, 5,000+ users, 20+ paying businesses, peaked at #1 in Lifestyle on the Czech App Store (15 Aug 2026).

Backend & Infrastructure

  • REST API with 165 endpoints, 50 Prisma models and 74 PostgreSQL migrations, with geospatial queries driving proximity ranking and audience-location weighting in reward pricing
  • Ran 7 background workers in 2 environments across 14 Fargate services (payments, Stripe reconciliation, Instagram data collection, push and email delivery, maintenance), advisory-locked against rolling deploys
  • Shared the API contract as typed packages across the monorepo: 160 endpoint schemas and 160 SDK functions, Zod-validated on both ends, consumed by all three apps and the backend
  • Built passwordless auth: email OTP with attempt limits and lockouts, Apple and Google sign-in, JWTs scoped per account type, and Redis rate limits shared across instances
  • Shipped everything from GitHub Actions (CI/CD): the API, 7 workers, app builds and 3 websites

Payments

  • Designed a double-entry ledger with 16 transaction kinds: deposits, withdrawals, refunds, share payments, referral payouts, locks, unlocks and adjustments
  • Every movement is one immutable row across six delta columns, with internal transfers aborting unless the deltas sum to zero; settlement is idempotent on a composite external reference, race-catching concurrent webhook replays
  • Integrated Stripe both directions: PaymentSheet and off-session card charges in, Connect Express payouts out, platform and Connect webhooks, with a reconciliation worker as delivery backstop
  • Built a 9-multiplier pricing engine computing payout value from live Instagram reach, audience geography and demographics, posting frequency and account quality

Mobile

  • Built three React Native apps from scratch: the customer and business apps are live on the App Store and Google Play, the admin app ships internally through TestFlight
  • Built the components and design tokens shared by all three apps, so a design change only happens once
  • Owned the release pipeline: EAS Build/Submit via GitHub Actions, three environments per app, TestFlight and Play Internal Testing, iOS credentials and Play App Signing
  • Attributed installs from physical QR codes using AppsFlyer: a per-sticker ID resolves to a business on our join page, then survives the store install via deferred deep links to auto-attribute on first open
  • Restructured onboarding after PostHog funnel tracking showed low completion: replaced Instagram-first sign-up with email OTP + Apple and Google OAuth, which doubled the completion rate

Stopped in Sep 2026: with around 20 businesses the marketplace never became active, so we couldn't raise a pre-seed round to grow it.
